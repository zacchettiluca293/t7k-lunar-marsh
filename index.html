How your number is calculated

Your target is annual income × a safe multiple. The multiple depends on how long retirement lasts (life expectancy minus the age you stop working):

~30 year retirement25x · 4.0% withdrawal
~40 year retirement28x · 3.5% withdrawal
~50 year retirement31x · 3.25% withdrawal

Why retiring younger needs more: if you stop at 45 and live to 90, your savings must survive 45 years of withdrawals and market crashes. The lower withdrawal rate protects against running out if a downturn hits early in retirement.

The trade-off, in plain terms: retire at 45 and every year is yours, but you need a bigger cushion and decades of discipline first. Retire at 60 and the target is smaller and safer, but you trade away your healthiest free years. The slider lets you see exactly what each choice costs.
